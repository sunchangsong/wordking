# WordKing — Make Bulgarian Vocabulary Learning More Efficient and Sustainable

When learning Bulgarian, memorizing words is not an isolated task. It is the foundation for improving listening, speaking, reading, and writing. The more stable your vocabulary is, the easier it becomes to understand sentences, read articles, follow conversations, and express ideas. Many learning bottlenecks are not caused by grammar alone, but by slow vocabulary recall when users see, hear, or want to use a word.

**WordKing** uses scientific review, categorized learning, varied practice, and clear progress feedback to help Bulgarian learners turn words from short-term memory into long-term mastery.

---

## Why Vocabulary Memorization Matters for Learning Bulgarian

### Vocabulary is the entry point to understanding
- A larger vocabulary means fewer interruptions when reading Bulgarian materials
- Strong high-frequency words make it easier to catch key information while listening
- Example sentences help users understand how words are used in real context
- A stable vocabulary base makes grammar and sentence patterns easier to absorb

### Memorizing words improves expression
- Users can find suitable Bulgarian words faster when they want to speak
- Writing becomes more accurate and less repetitive
- Common collocations and phrases make spoken language more natural
- Words gradually move from passive recognition to active speaking and writing

### Scientific review builds long-term memory
- Learning only new words leads to quick forgetting
- Review based on forgetting patterns reduces wasted repetition
- Mistake-prone and weak words receive more attention
- Small daily steps are better for language growth than irregular cramming

## WordKing Features

### Smart vocabulary learning
WordKing helps users learn Bulgarian vocabulary with a plan, moving from basic words to advanced expressions step by step. Meanings, examples, pronunciation, and practice help users understand words instead of mechanically memorizing translations.

### Scientific review
WordKing schedules review according to learning and mastery status. Mastered words receive less repetition, while weak words get more practice opportunities.

### Multi-scenario practice
- Word recognition for first memory
- Spelling practice for accurate output
- Pronunciation and listening practice to connect sound and meaning
- Example sentence learning for real usage
- Review tests to check mastery

### Learning data feedback
WordKing records progress, review status, and mastery. Users can clearly see how many words they learned, which words are mastered, and which words still need reinforcement.
